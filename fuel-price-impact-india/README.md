# 🚀 Impact of Fuel Prices on Transportation Costs in India

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes how fuel prices (petrol & diesel) impact transportation costs in India using crude oil prices and inflation data as supporting indicators.

## 🎯 Objective

* Analyze fuel price trends over time
* Understand relationship between diesel prices and inflation
* Study impact of crude oil on fuel prices

## 📊 Data Sources

* Fuel Prices (Petrol & Diesel)
* Crude Oil Prices
* Inflation Data (CPI)
* Time Period: 2017 – 2025

## 🛠 Tools Used

* Excel (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
* Power BI (Data Visualization & Dashboard)

## 📈 Key Insights

* Fuel prices closely follow crude oil trends
* Diesel has a strong impact on inflation due to transportation usage
* Fuel prices did not decrease proportionately during COVID
* Government policies and taxes influence fuel pricing
* Overall upward trend in fuel prices over time

Fuel prices, especially diesel, significantly influence transportation costs and indirectly affect inflation and the overall economy.
